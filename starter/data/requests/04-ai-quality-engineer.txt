AI Quality Engineer – Customer Support Assistant

Sundnet, a Nordic telecom operator, has launched an AI assistant that helps customers
with invoices, coverage and subscription changes. It answers thousands of questions a
day, and the team now needs someone who can tell when it gets things wrong before the
customers do.

In this role you will design how the assistant is tested and evaluated. You will build
test suites for the chat flows, set up automated evaluations of the model's answers and
make sure they run on every change. You will work closely with the developers who build
the assistant and with customer service, who know which answers matter most. Expect to
spend time on both classic test automation and new ways of measuring the quality of
generated text.

You probably have several years of experience in test automation and are comfortable
writing tests in Python or TypeScript. You have used tools like Playwright and know how
to make tests part of a CI pipeline. You have tried prompt engineering and are curious
about evaluating language models, even if you have not done it in production yet.

It is a plus if you have worked with telecom systems, built evaluation datasets, or
used frameworks for agents or LLM evaluation.

Hybrid in Stockholm, start in February, initially for 6 months.
