# Senior Fullstack Engineer – Partner Portal

**Company:** Ferrylane (online booking of ferry and train trips)
**Location:** Gothenburg, hybrid · **Start:** January 2027 · **Duration:** 9 months, likely extension

## The team

The Partner team owns the portal where ferry operators and rail companies manage their
routes, prices and availability. The portal has grown quickly and now needs a solid
backend for bulk updates and a frontend that operators actually enjoy using.

## Your mission

You will work across the stack, from React screens to the services that push changes
to our booking engine. A typical week can include designing an API with the team,
building a new import flow for timetables, and tracking down why a price update did not
reach the search results.

## You bring

- Around six years or more of building and running web applications in production.
- Strong React and TypeScript.
- Backend experience in Java or Kotlin, for example with Spring Boot.
- Experience with event-driven systems, preferably Kafka.
- Comfort with cloud infrastructure, preferably AWS.
- Good communication with both colleagues and external partners.

## Bonus points

Next.js, Playwright or other end-to-end testing, PostgreSQL, and experience from travel
or e-commerce.

## Good to know

The team is five engineers, a designer and a product manager. About two thirds of the
work is frontend right now, since the backend is in decent shape.
